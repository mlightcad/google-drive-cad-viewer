Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026 · Product: Google Drive CAD Viewer (MLightCAD)

This Privacy Policy describes how Google Drive CAD Viewer (“the App”) accesses, uses, and handles Google user data when you connect your Google account. The App is provided by MLightCAD and is intended for viewing CAD drawings (DWG/DXF) from Google Drive in your browser.

Google user data we access

With your consent, the App requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — access only to Drive files that you open with this App (Google Picker or Drive “Open with”), not your entire Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile — to show your account name, email, and profile picture in the App UI.

How we use Google user data

  • Download the CAD file you select so it can be displayed in the in-browser viewer (MLightCAD embed).
  • Show which Google account is signed in.

We use Google user data only to provide these user-facing features. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness.

Storage, sharing, and transfer

  • File content is downloaded in your browser using your Google access token and passed to the viewer embed via postMessage. The App does not upload your Drive files to our servers for storage.
  • Access tokens are kept in the browser session for Google API calls; signing out revokes the token when possible.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to operate the viewer experience in your browser, or when required by law or for security investigations.

Limited Use

The App’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • You can disconnect the App at any time using Sign Out in the App.
  • You can also revoke access in your Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the App: mlight.lee@outlook.com · mlightcad.com · GitHub issues.
